# DevFest 2025 - GDG WOW Pune

## Event Details

**Event:** DevFest 2025 - Women in Tech Special Edition  
**Date:** September 15-16, 2025  
**Venue:** JW Marriott, Senapati Bapat Road, Pune  
**Theme:** "AI Innovation & Inclusion"

## About DevFest 2025

DevFest 2025 is GDG WOW Pune's flagship annual event, bringing together developers, designers, entrepreneurs, and tech enthusiasts for two days of learning, networking, and inspiration. This year's edition focuses on Artificial Intelligence innovations while emphasizing inclusion and diversity in the tech industry.
