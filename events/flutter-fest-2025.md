# Flutter Fest 2025

## Event Overview

**Date:** May 15-16, 2025  
**Time:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM  
**Location:** Tech Hub Convention Center, Baner, Pune  
**Format:** In-person conference with livestream option

## About the Event

Flutter Fest 2025 is GDG WOW Pune's premier event focused on Flutter development and women in technology. This two-day conference brings together Flutter enthusiasts, from beginners to experts, to share knowledge, network, and collaborate on exciting projects. With a special emphasis on empowering women in the Flutter ecosystem, this event promises to be an enriching experience for all participants.

## Event Highlights

- **Keynote by Google Flutter Team**: Exclusive insights into Flutter's roadmap and latest features
- **Technical Workshops**: Hands-on sessions on Flutter 4.0, Firebase integration, and advanced UI/UX design
- **Code Labs**: Guided coding sessions for all experience levels
- **Women in Tech Panel**: Discussion on navigating and excelling in the tech industry
- **Project Showcase**: Demonstrations of innovative Flutter applications
- **Networking Opportunities**: Connect with Flutter developers, potential employers, and mentors
- **Job Fair**: Meet companies hiring Flutter developers
